# Big Blue Mop Content Operating System v1.0

## Purpose

This document exists to ensure every AI, contractor, designer, marketer, copywriter, automation platform, or content creator produces content that is consistent with the Big Blue Mop brand.

This is not a suggestion.

These are operating instructions.

If there is a conflict between creativity and these instructions, these instructions win.

---

## Core Positioning

Most cleaning companies sell cleaning.

Big Blue Mop sells trust.

Cleaning is the vehicle.

Trust is the product.

The customer is not buying a cleaner.

The customer is buying confidence that they do not have to think about cleaning anymore.

The meta problem is not dirt.

The meta problem is having to check.

Examples:

- Checking whether bins were emptied.
- Checking whether bathrooms were cleaned.
- Checking whether consumables were refilled.
- Chasing replies.
- Repeating the same issue.
- Following up on unresolved problems.

The moment the customer feels compelled to check, trust has already been damaged.

---

## Brand Philosophy

Big Blue Mop gives people permission to admit what they already know.

We are not educating.

We are not inspiring.

We are not motivating.

We are not entertaining.

We are validating.

The audience already knows something is wrong.

The content helps them stop talking themselves out of it.

---

## Voice

Speak like a frustrated business owner.

Not a marketer.

Not a consultant.

Not a coach.

Not a motivational speaker.

Not a cleaning company.

Use simple language.

Use direct language.

Use everyday language.

Use conversational language.

The audience should feel:

> Finally somebody said it.

---

## Forbidden Language

Do not use:

- Industry jargon
- Corporate language
- Consultant language
- Leadership lessons
- Generic business advice
- Inspirational quotes
- Motivational cliches

Avoid:

- Trust is built...
- People make mistakes...
- At what point do you...
- Research shows...
- Studies indicate...
- Best practice suggests...

Do not sound clever.

Do not sound polished.

Do not sound professional.

Sound truthful.

---

## Core Framework

Every piece of content follows this sequence.

### Step 1: Thumb on the bruise

Name the thing they are already seeing.

### Step 2: Why are you accepting this?

Challenge the rationalisation.

### Step 3: What are you going to do about it?

Force a decision.

## Bad Examples

```text

```

```text
Communication is important.
```

```text

```

```text

```

Big Blue Mop points.

## Graphic Template Rules

Non negotiable:

- One premise per image.
- One idea per image.
- One image equals one social post.
- Never combine multiple premises.
- Never create collages.
- Never create multi panel graphics.
- Never create carousels unless specifically requested.

---

## Image Specifications

Background:

- Solid black.
- No gradients.
- No textures.
- No photos.
- No patterns.
- No shadows.
- No visual clutter.

---

## Logo Rules

Use original Big Blue Mop logo only.

Never:

- Redesign.
- Redraw.
- Reinterpret.
- Crop.
- Partially display.
- Change colours.
- Stretch.
- Recreate with AI.

Logo position:

- Top centre.
- Entire logo visible.
- Large safety margin above and around logo.
- Logo must never touch image edge.
- Text must never overlap the logo.

---

## Brand Colour

Official Big Blue Mop Blue:

```text
HEX: #5FACDB
RGB: 95, 172, 219
```

This colour is the source of truth.

Never:

- Darken.
- Brighten.
- Oversaturate.
- Substitute.
- Use a similar blue.

All blue text must use `#5FACDB`.

---

## Typography

- Large.
- Bold.
- Readable.
- Mobile first.
- High contrast.
- White and `#5FACDB` only.
- Blue should be used as emphasis.
- Do not make every word blue.
- Do not make every line blue.
- Use blue strategically.

---

## CTA

Always:

```text

```

Nothing else.

No phone number.

No website.

No additional CTA.

---

## Social Caption Rules

The image already contains the premise.

Do not repeat the premise in the caption.

The caption must continue the conversation.

Framework:

1. Confirm the problem.
2. Challenge acceptance.
3. Prompt action.

Caption rules:

- Short.
- Direct.
- Punchy.
- No essays.
- No explanations.
- No teaching.
- No storytelling.
- No poetry.
- No corporate polish.

## Production Workflow For AI Agents

When asked to create Big Blue Mop social content:

1. Read this document first.
2. Identify the single premise.
3. Create one image only.
4. Use the locked template.
5. Use the original Big Blue Mop logo.
6. Use black background.
7. Use only white and `#5FACDB`.
8. Keep all text inside safe margins.
9. Use `CHECK LINK IN BIO` as the only CTA.
10. Do not add icons, boxes, borders, QR codes, phone numbers, website links, feature lists, or extra design elements.
11. Do not create collages.
12. Do not put multiple premises on one image.
13. Do not rewrite locked copy unless explicitly asked.
14. Do not create a new framework.
15. Do not explain the work unless asked.

Ask:

Does this sound like a consultant?

If yes, rewrite.

If yes, rewrite.

Does this sound like a frustrated business owner finally saying what everyone is already thinking?
